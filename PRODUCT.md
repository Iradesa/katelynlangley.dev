## Platform

## Users

The portfolio is for anyone interested in Katelyn Langley's projects and development work, including people evaluating her for software roles.

## Product Purpose

This personal portfolio showcases what Katelyn has built, learned, and accomplished. Success means helping any visitor understand her capabilities through concrete work, with professional opportunities emerging naturally from that evidence rather than explicit job-seeking language.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents a developer who turns foundational knowledge, collaborative experience, continued study, and AI-assisted workflows into deployed software and playable projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive through general project discovery, professional networking, recruiting, or hiring evaluation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The primary call to action is direct email at `katie@wyrd.team`.
- The site is a personal portfolio and must not state that it is owned by Wyrd or another company.
- No login, about, privacy-policy, or terms-of-use pages are required.
- The project uses Astro and Tailwind CSS.
- Work leads the narrative while the About copy clearly states that Katelyn is searching for full-time Junior Software Developer work.
- Confirmed skills include Adobe Photoshop, AI-agent workflows, C++, CSS, Docker, Figma, GitHub, Go, HTML, Java, JavaScript, Python, Unity 5, and Unreal Engine.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Katelyn Langley.
- Personal, capable, candid tone.
- The site should sound like Katelyn speaking to a curious visitor: warm, direct, enthusiastic about learning, and never résumé-like or corporate.
- Purple and green are preferred colors, without restricting every exploration to the same palette.
